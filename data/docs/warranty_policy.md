# New vehicle warranty

All Norvik vehicles sold in the EU come with a 3-year or 100,000 km new vehicle warranty, whichever comes first. It covers defects in materials and workmanship.

# High-voltage battery warranty

The high-voltage battery (hybrid or EV) is covered for 8 years or 160,000 km, whichever comes first. For the Tern EV, the battery is also covered if usable capacity falls below 70 percent within that period. Capacity loss above 70 percent is considered normal ageing.

# Conditions

Warranty coverage requires servicing according to the maintenance schedule, at a Norvik dealer or an independent workshop that uses parts of equivalent quality and keeps records. Damage from accidents, flooding, unapproved modifications, or using the wrong coolant or fluids is not covered.

# Wear items not covered

Brake pads, brake discs, wiper blades, tyres, and the 12 V battery after the first 2 years are wear items and are not covered by the new vehicle warranty.

# Making a claim

Warranty claims must be made through a Norvik dealer. The customer should bring the service booklet or digital service record. The dealer diagnoses the fault and submits the claim; approval normally takes 2 to 5 working days.

# Goodwill

Outside the warranty period, Norvik may offer goodwill contributions for failures shortly after expiry, decided case by case by the dealer and the regional after-sales manager.
