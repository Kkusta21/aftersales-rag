# Aster Hybrid schedule

Every 15,000 km or 12 months: engine oil and filter, cabin air filter, visual brake check, hybrid system scan. Every 30,000 km: engine air filter. Every 60,000 km: spark plugs. Every 2 years: brake fluid.

# Tern EV schedule

Every 20,000 km or 12 months: cabin air filter, tyre rotation check, brake check, high-voltage system scan. Every 2 years: brake fluid. Every 4 years: air conditioning service.

# Service booking

Services can be booked in the Norvik app under Service > Book, or by calling the dealer. A courtesy car can be requested at booking but is subject to availability.
