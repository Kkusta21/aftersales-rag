# Engine oil

The Aster Hybrid uses a 1.6 L petrol engine. Use 0W-20 fully synthetic oil meeting ACEA C5. Oil capacity with filter change is 4.2 litres. Change the oil every 15,000 km or 12 months, whichever comes first. Vehicles used mainly for short trips under 10 km, towing, or in dusty conditions should change oil every 7,500 km.

# Tyre pressure

Recommended cold tyre pressure is 2.4 bar front and 2.3 bar rear for 17-inch wheels. For 18-inch wheels use 2.5 bar front and 2.4 bar rear. The label is on the driver's door pillar. After adjusting pressures, reset the TPMS from Settings > Vehicle > Tyres > Calibrate and drive for 20 minutes above 25 km/h.

# 12 V battery

The 12 V auxiliary battery is in the boot, under the left side trim panel. If the vehicle shows "READY" does not appear and the dashboard is dim, the 12 V battery is the most likely cause. Jump-start terminals are under the bonnet in the fuse box, marked with a red cover. Never connect jump leads directly to the high-voltage battery.

# Hybrid system warning light

A yellow hybrid system warning means the car can still be driven but should be inspected within 7 days. A red hybrid system warning means stop safely as soon as possible, switch off, and call Norvik Roadside Assistance. Do not open the orange high-voltage cables.

# Brake fluid

Use DOT 4 brake fluid. Replace every 2 years regardless of mileage. Regenerative braking reduces pad wear, so pads often last beyond 80,000 km, but the brake discs can corrode if the friction brakes are rarely used. Owners who drive gently should brake firmly a few times a month.

# Error code P0A80

P0A80 means "replace hybrid battery pack" and is stored when the cell block voltage difference exceeds the limit. The dealer must run the battery health check (procedure HV-12) before replacement. Hybrid battery replacement is covered under the high-voltage battery warranty if the conditions are met.
