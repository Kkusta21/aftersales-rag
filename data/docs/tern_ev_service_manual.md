# Charging

The Tern EV supports AC charging up to 11 kW and DC fast charging up to 150 kW through the CCS2 port. A DC charge from 10 to 80 percent takes about 28 minutes at a 150 kW charger with the battery preconditioned. Battery preconditioning starts automatically when a fast charger is set as the navigation destination.

# Charging fault: charge port light flashing red

A flashing red charge port light means the charging session was stopped by a fault. First check that the cable is fully latched, then try another charger. If the fault repeats on two different chargers, the on-board charger or the port lock actuator may be faulty. Book a dealer inspection and quote code CHG-07.

# Tyre pressure

Recommended cold tyre pressure is 2.7 bar front and rear on 19-inch wheels. The Tern EV is heavier than a petrol car of the same size, so tyres wear faster; rotate them every 10,000 km.

# Cabin air filter

Replace the cabin air filter every 20,000 km or 12 months. It is behind the glovebox. The filter is a PM2.5 type, part number TRN-CF-221.

# Coolant for the battery thermal system

The battery thermal system uses a dedicated low-conductivity coolant, part number TRN-LC-40. Never top up with standard engine coolant. Replacement interval is 200,000 km or 10 years.

# Software updates

Over-the-air updates are installed from Settings > Software. The car must be parked, with at least 30 percent charge, and cannot be driven during installation, which takes up to 45 minutes. Some recall remedies are delivered as over-the-air updates.
